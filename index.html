    Formula lab

    A prediction formula you can tune. It gives every number a score from the past results, turns the scores into chances, draws a set, and throws away any set that has already come up. Then it replays the whole year to see whether the formula would have beaten chance.

    The formula

    1. Frequency, with Bayesian shrinkage. fi = (ci + a) / (N + a·M), where ci is how often number i was drawn, N is all numbers drawn, M is the highest number and a is the prior. A large a pulls every number back towards fair when there's little data.
    2. Recency. ri = e−gi/τ, where gi is the number of draws since i last appeared. A positive weight favours numbers seen recently, and a negative weight favours overdue ones.
    3. Sheet group heat. hi is the average recent hits of the groups on your sheet that contain i.
    4. Score. Si = wf·z(fi) + wr·z(ri) + wh·z(hi), where z(·) rescales each part to mean 0 and standard deviation 1 so the weights compare fairly.
    5. Chance of picking i. Pi = eSi/T / Σj eSj/T (a softmax). A low temperature T makes the formula stick to its top scores, and a high T makes it close to random.
    6. No repeats. The formula draws numbers from Pi without replacement. It rejects the set if every number in it appeared together in any past draw, or if the set shares more than the allowed amount with the last draw.
